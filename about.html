About

The studio, the work, and the people behind it.

The Studio

PhasmoFire Studios is an independent game development studio focused on creating memorable experiences — games that stick with you long after you've put the controller down.

Every project here is built by a single developer with a passion for both the technical and creative sides of game dev. From concept art to code, everything is handcrafted.

"If there is one thing I have always loved, it was puzzles. Coding is like a giant puzzle to me. Fitting each line like a puzzle piece and seeing what does and doesn't fit or what needs to be moved to a different spot or even what pieces belong to a different puzzle altogether. Games are just a giant puzzle to me and I think it is fun." - Phasmo Fire God

"I just really like music, it's like music to my ears, ya know?" - IGreatlyAdmireCats
